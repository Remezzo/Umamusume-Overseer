> **Download `Overseer.exe` → double-click → play.** It finds your game on any Steam drive, installs itself, opens its dashboard, and updates itself on every new release. No Python. No extracting. No instructions to read. If you can double-click, you're done.

Overseer is a **passive companion** — it watches the game and hands you superpowers, but it **never plays for you**. Every choice stays yours; Overseer just makes sure it's an informed one.

---

## 🗣️ Translation that actually reads like the game

**The entire game, in your language, fully offline.** Not screenshots, not OCR, not a browser tab on your second monitor — Overseer replaces the game's text *inside the game*, sourced from the game's **own strings**, so every stat is exact to the digit and no character's name ever comes out as gibberish.

- **French and German ship in the download** — 54,000+ and 45,000+ translated lines, menus to story to live race commentary, working the moment you pick them. No internet required, ever.
- **25+ more languages** via an on-device neural engine — everything is translated on *your* machine. Nothing you read is ever sent anywhere.
- **English on the Japanese client, end-to-end.** Play the version that gets content first — and read all of it.
- **It learns as you play.** Every line translated once appears instantly forever after. Disagree with a wording? Fix it once and Overseer remembers your version permanently.
- **A protected glossary** locks the terms that must be right — names stay names, dates stay dates, skills mean what they say.

*Other tools translate a screenshot of the game. Overseer translates the game.*

## 🔮 Predictions — know it before you click

The game already decided what happens. Overseer reads that decision **before you commit to it.**

- **Every event choice, decoded.** What each option *really* gives — stats, skills, energy, hidden branches — with the best pick highlighted before you tap. And when an outcome is a genuine dice-roll, Overseer says "this one's a gamble" instead of pretending — verified honesty, no false confidence.
- **Your race result, forecast at the entry screen.** See your projected finishing place while you can still do something about it.
- **Every training tile, quantified.** Exact gains, skill-hint procs, and the **real failure chance** — so a 73%-fail trap never eats your turn again.
- **A full Deck Builder** on the game's own database — effects breakdown, skill analysis, training gains, rainbow odds. Your owned cards load automatically; theorycraft with everything else.

*Stop guessing. The data was always there — Overseer just shows it to you in time.*

## ⏩ Skips & speed — respect for your time

You've watched that training cut-in four hundred times. Overseer gives you the hours back.

- **One-tap native skips** for events, training animations, race results and the shop — driven by the game's **own** skip and fast-forward routines, so they're instant, safe, and never desync the UI.
- **Animation speed up to 20×** — menus, transitions and event text at whatever pace you can read.
- **Races fast-forward only when you've won.** Lose, or an unknown result? It stops and hands you the controls. It never touches Team Trials.
- **Auto-advance** for warnings, inspiration events and skill confirms — every one individually toggleable, every one off by default.
- **Performance unlocked:** cap the frame rate anywhere from 1–300 or uncap it entirely, force max 3D quality — or flip **Low Resource mode** and run the whole game smoothly on a potato.

*A career that took an evening takes a coffee break.*

## 🧭 The Live Advisor — a coach on every turn

Overseer doesn't just show you data — it reads the turn and **tells you what it would do.**

- **The recommended event choice, highlighted in the sidebar** the moment the event appears — backed by the same decoded outcomes you can inspect yourself.
- **Risk called out in plain language.** Low energy with a high-fail training on screen? The advisor says **"Rest and recover first — this fails 73% of the time"** instead of letting you walk into it.
- **A live rail on every page** — energy, mood, stats, the current call and the reasoning behind it — that follows you around the dashboard and pops out into its own window beside a fullscreen game.
- **Advice only, always.** It recommends; you decide. Nothing is ever clicked for you unless you explicitly turn an auto-option on.
- The advisor's deep training engine — full turn-by-turn "train this, here's why" across every scenario — is powered by **Icarus**, our career automation engine, and comes online automatically when it's present. The predictions, event recommendations and risk warnings above are **built in** and work out of the box.

*It's the difference between playing with the wiki open and playing with a coach watching your screen.*

## And the rest of the dashboard

**Veterans** roster with inheritance sparks · **career tracking** with leaderboards and history · **Discord webhooks** for finished careers · **AI Brain** learning from your own runs · **accessibility** (reduce motion, high contrast, text scaling, colour-vision modes) · **one-file diagnostic export** when you need help — all from one clean dashboard at `127.0.0.1:1620`.

## Private by architecture

Everything runs **on your PC**. The dashboard is localhost-only, translation is on-device, and your data never leaves your machine. Overseer never touches your account server-side and never automates play — it's an unofficial companion that improves what *you* see and do, not a bot playing in your place.

## Install

1. **[Download `Overseer-v1.0.0.exe`](../../releases/latest)** — one file, everything bundled.
2. **Double-click it.** (Windows SmartScreen? *More info → Run anyway.* Antivirus flagging the overlay loader once is a known false-positive — allow-list the game folder.)
3. That's it. The game launches, the dashboard opens, and every feature above is a switch away.

**Requirements:** Windows 10/11 · Umamusume: Pretty Derby on Steam (Global or Japanese) · Windowed/Borderless mode. No runtimes, no dev tools, no internet needed for the built-in languages.
